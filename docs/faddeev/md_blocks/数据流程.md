```text
[Input: CLI + Input/*.txt]
        |
        v
[Build model space]
  - choose potential (Nijmegen, N3LO, ...)
  - choose partial waves (Jmax, channels)
  - choose energies ELab grid
        |
        v
[WPCD discretization]
  - define p-bins and q-bins (NWP)
  - build wave-packet basis |x>, |x̄>, |X>=|x>⊗|x̄>
  - normalization / weights
        |
        v
[Two-body ingredients]
  - compute v1 / t-matrix related objects in WP basis
  - build channel resolvent G1(E) (analytic/closed-form in WPCD)
        |
        v
[Permutation operator]
  - build P in the (partial-wave ⊗ WP) basis
  - cache P (independent of LECs / potentials parameters)
        |
        v
[Assemble AGS kernel]
  U = P v1 + P v1 G1 U
        |
        v
[Solve]
  - Neumann series: U = Σ_n (P v1 G1)^n (P v1)
  - Padé resummation to handle divergence (Weinberg eigenvalues > 1)
        |
        v
[On-shell projection]
  - extract on-shell nd amplitudes (U-matrix elements)
  - (optional) compute observables vs angle/energy
        |
        v
[Output]
  - HDF5 files (U-matrix, observables, metadata)
```
